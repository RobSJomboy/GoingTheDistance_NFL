FORCE A RESULT
Safe havens at the 25, the 50 and the opponent 20 — the Millionaire guarantee. Turn it over on downs and the next drive starts from the last one you got past, not back at the goal line.

3 Head to head

Right answer moves the ball your way and you keep it. Wrong answer is a turnover on the spot and the other side takes over driving the other way. Reach the end zone for 7 and it goes back to the 50.

3 Two-minute drill one clock for the whole round, not per question

Questions come back to back off one running clock. A right answer moves the ball by how fast it came; a wrong one costs ten seconds and no yards. Score and the ball goes back to the 20 and you keep going. When the clock hits zero the round is whatever you banked.

3 Sudden death one wrong answer ends it

Every question is the whole season. Right and you keep driving, wrong and it is over on the spot — the run is however far you got and however many you strung together. Best run of the taping is tracked next to the current one.

3 The wager call the yardage before the question goes up

Set the wager before serving — it goes on the overlay with the question so the room can see what is at stake. Right answer gains it, wrong answer loses it, and a wager that would take you past the goal line is trimmed to the distance left.
