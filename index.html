GOING THE DISTANCE

An NFL trivia drive. You start on your own 1 and answer your way to the end zone — and how fast you answer decides what the play is. Fire back instantly and you take the top off it. Sit on it and you scramble for two. Miss it and you're eating a sack.

Five games, three difficulties, and an era filter so a round can be all 90s or all 2020s. 373 questions in the bank, every one of them with a citation — or write your own.

TWO WAYS TO RUN IT

On your own: open Play and go. Everything happens in your browser — nothing to connect, nothing to install.

As a show: a host drives the Control page and the 8-bit field goes out to an OBS browser source. Both run the same rules off the same code, so a game plays the same either way.

  1. Open Control, pick a topic name, hit Connect.
  2. Copy Display URL and paste it into an OBS browser source — 1920 × 1080 for the lower third, 1080 × 1920 for the vertical cut.
  3. Pick the game, the difficulty and which eras are in play.
  4. Hit N to serve a question, read it out, click the answer they gave, then Enter to reveal and run the play.
  5. Drop your own audio clips on the control page and they replace the built-in stingers.
Built for Talkin' Baseball's football cousin. Both pages talk over a public ntfy topic, so the laptop running the show and the machine running OBS never have to be on the same network.
