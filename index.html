RUNNING IT

  1. Open Control, pick a topic name, hit Connect.
  2. Copy Display URL and paste it into an OBS browser source — 1920 × 1080 for the lower third, 1080 × 1920 for the vertical cut.
  3. Hit N to serve a question, read it out, click the answer they gave, then Enter to reveal and run the play.
  4. Drop your own audio clips on the control page and they replace the built-in stingers.
